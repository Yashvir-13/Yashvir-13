### / OBSERVE

I write software to study perception, representation, and the boundary between human and machine interpretation. My work sits at the intersection where physical reality is translated into machine representation. I am less interested in the black box, and more in the geometry of the space inside it.

<br>

- **Focus:** Intelligent Systems / Computer Vision / Representation
- **State:** Experimenting with classical constraints in modern architectures
- **Stack:** Python, PyTorch, C/C++, OpenCV
